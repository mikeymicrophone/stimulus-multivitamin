Oma Ilse’s plum cake

Serves 10

  • 2 pounds Italian prune plums, halved
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup cold butter
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Shared by Greta in Milwaukee. Pack the plums upright and tight; they shrink as they bake.

Smoky black bean chili

Serves 6

  • 2 cans black beans, drained
  • 1 chipotle in adobo, minced
  • 1 can fire-roasted tomatoes
  • 1 ½ teaspoons smoked paprika
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro

Shared by Ramón in Tucson. Better on day two; it freezes for three months.
