Recipe card collection

A home cook's digital recipe box. Every card is a recipe-card controller that scales its own servings. Editing a card attaches a recipe-editor controller to that card only for as long as you are editing.

Rosa's recipe box

Weeknight red lentil dal

Serves 4

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 3 cups water
  • ½ teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 tablespoons ghee

Bloom the cumin in the ghee until it crackles, then pour it over the dal right before serving.

Brown butter cornbread

Serves 8

  • 1 cup coarse cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup brown butter, cooled
  • 1 ¼ cups buttermilk
  • 2 eggs

Heat the cast-iron skillet in the oven while it preheats so the edges crisp.

Charred scallion pancakes

Serves 4

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup just-boiled water
  • 6 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil

Rest the dough for 30 minutes; it rolls thinner and puffs into flaky layers.
