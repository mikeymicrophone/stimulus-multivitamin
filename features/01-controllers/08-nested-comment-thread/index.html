Tomatoes splitting after heavy rain. What am I doing wrong?

Posted by Marisol (plot 22) in Allotment Commons · Vegetables ·

Every time we get a big storm, half my Early Girls crack open around the stem the next morning. I water every evening. Is it the soil, the variety, or me?

Hollis (plot 12) · 5h ago

Splitting after a downpour is almost always uneven watering. The fruit swells faster than the skin can stretch. Mulch heavily and water deeply twice a week instead of a little every day.

Beatriz (plot 7) · 4h ago

Seconding the mulch. I switched to straw last summer and my Brandywines stopped cracking almost completely.

Hollis (plot 12) · 4h ago

Straw is great as long as it is seed-free. Wheat straw from the feed store sprouted all over mine one year.

Beatriz (plot 7) · 3h ago

Good tip. I buy the bagged stuff labeled weed-free now.

Dev (plot 3) · 3h ago

Also worth picking anything that is blushing before a big storm. They finish ripening fine on the windowsill.

Old Tom (plot 1) · 2h ago

Variety matters too. Sun Gold and the other cherry types split if you look at them wrong. Try Juliet or Mountain Merit next year; both are crack-resistant.

Ana (plot 19) · 1h ago

Juliet has been bulletproof for me, even through the August storms.
