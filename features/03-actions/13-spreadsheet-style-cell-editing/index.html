Spreadsheet-style cell editing

The weekly harvest board at a CSA farm. Each cell is an action. Click a crate count to edit it, then Tab, Enter, or the arrow keys to move, or click elsewhere to commit. New crops, whether typed or fetched from last week, arrive with the same actions already on every cell.
