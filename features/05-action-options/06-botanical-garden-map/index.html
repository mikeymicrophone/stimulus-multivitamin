Trail notes

The east path from the palm court to the alpine pit is crushed granite and takes a wheelchair. Benches sit under the catalpa at 40 metres.

Docent tours leave the lobby at 11:00 and 14:00. Sunday's 14:00 is full.

The fernery misting cycle runs at :15 past each hour. The rill is slick then.

Lost children go to the kiosk by the orchid house, not the lobby.

Tonight the conservatory closes at 17:30 for a members' preview of the gentian trays.
