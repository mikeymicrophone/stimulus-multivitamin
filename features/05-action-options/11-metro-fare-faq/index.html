Metro fare FAQ

Harbor Metro's fare guide. Each topic is a <details> with toggle->fare-guide#announceOpen:open and announceClosed:!open. The custom option's callback logs every argument it receives.

Harbor Metro · Fares and passes

Adult fares

A single ride is $2.75. A day pass is $11, sold on the bus or in the Harbor Street kiosk.

Youth fares

Riders 18 and under ride for $1.25. A school term pass is $70, issued at Millbrook High and Harbor Metro customer service.

Reduced fares

Medicare cards and Harbor Metro reduced-fare IDs pay $1.10. Apply at the customer service window on Harbor Street.
