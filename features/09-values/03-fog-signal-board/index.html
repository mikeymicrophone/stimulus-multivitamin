Fog signal board

Morning fog off the Strait. Each station phoned its horn status in a different hand: FALSE, a zero, a blank slip, a trailing space.

Point Wilson

Slip says false, and the watch is manned.

Ediz Hook

Sounding was logged as 0. The manned column says 00.

Marrowstone

The keeper wrote FALSE, and "no" in the manned column.

Dungeness

The sounding cell was left blank. Manned says false.

Smith Island

No sounding was phoned in. The watch marked manned as 1.

Protection Island

The sounding slip has a trailing space. Manned says off.
